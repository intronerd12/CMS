Privacy Policy & Academic Integrity

1. Data Protection: Your account information (name, email, and password) is stored in a local encrypted database and is only used for course administration and grade records.

2. Assessment Integrity: All quiz submissions and timestamps are securely logged. Question order and multiple-choice options are dynamically shuffled to ensure fair assessment for every student.

3. Academic Records: Quiz scores, passing verdicts, and official grade slips are verifiable by faculty and the department chair.
