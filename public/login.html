Protected by Institutional Academic Integrity • Privacy Policy & Academic Terms

Privacy Policy & Academic Integrity (Item 1)

1. Data Protection: Your account information (name, email, role, and encrypted password) is stored in a secure local database and is only used for course administration and official grade records.

2. Assessment Integrity & Anti-Cheating: All quiz submissions, question shuffle states, and timestamps are securely logged. Question order and multiple-choice options are dynamically shuffled to ensure fair and randomized assessment for every student.

3. Role Privacy: Faculty have administrative access to manage subjects, author quizzes, and export grade matrix summaries. Students only access unlocked coursework and their individual grade records.

4. Academic Records: Official certificates and grade slips can be generated, printed, or exported as PDF by faculty and authorized students.
